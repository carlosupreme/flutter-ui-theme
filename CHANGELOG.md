# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

* Tokens: `UiTheme` (`UiColors` light, dark, AMOLED; `UiShape`; `UiMotion`;
  `UiTypography` with bundled Nunito; `UiEffects`) and `buildUiTheme`, which also
  restyles the stock Material widgets and installs the page transition.
* Buttons and controls: `UiPrimaryButton`, `UiSecondaryButton`, `UiPill`,
  `UiRoundButton`, `UiSegmentedToggle`, `UiSwitch`, `UiStepper`, `UiPressable`.
* Navigation: `UiShell`, `UiNavBar`, `UiNavRail`.
* Transitions: `UiScreenSwitcher`, `UiPageTransitionsBuilder`, `Rise`, `riseAll`,
  `UiRiseScope`.
* Surfaces, lists and inputs: `UiCard`, `UiGlassSurface`, `UiBadge`, `UiShimmer`,
  `UiPatternBackground`, `UiOptionGroup`, `UiListGroup`, `UiSearchField`,
  `UiScreenHeader`, `UiMarqueeText`, `UiRollingText`.
* Overlays: `showUiSheet` (adaptive), `showUiDialog`, `showUiConfirm`,
  `showUiToast`, `UiEdgeBlur`.
* Example gallery app with light, dark, AMOLED and an AA-checked clinic palette.
* 55 widget and unit tests.
